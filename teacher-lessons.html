Offline learning instructions

  1. Upload the video and PDF to Google Drive.
  2. Copy their share links into the form above.
  3. Publish the lesson while connected to the internet.
  4. Students open and download the actual files while online.
  5. Students can then open downloaded files offline.

Saving a link does not download the file. Students must download the actual file before losing internet access.
