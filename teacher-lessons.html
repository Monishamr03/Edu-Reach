Offline learning instructions

  1. Upload the video and PDF to Google Drive.
  2. Copy their share links into the form above.
  3. Publish the lesson.
  4. Students open and download the files while online.
  5. After downloading, students can open the saved files offline.

A link alone does not work offline. The actual file must be downloaded before the internet connection is lost.
